# Mercenary Name Shapes

An evaluation of 168 unique names, sourced from real `warrant.txt` shows every real mercenary name fits one of four shapes:

- **A** -- `Name, the Title` (1-word title) -- e.g. `Alton, the Turncoat` (93/168)
- **B** -- `Name, the Title Title` (2-word title) -- e.g. `Bib, the Azadin Howler` (37/168)
- **C** -- `Name Surname` (no comma, exactly 2 words) -- e.g. `Baknar Trosis` (35/168)
- **D** -- `Name, of Title Title[ Title]` (2-3 word title) -- e.g. `Oktor, of House Azadi` (3/168)

Rules holding across all four shapes, zero violations found:
- Every real word starts with a capital letter and is never uppercase
  beyond that first letter -- except that a hyphenated word's SECOND
  half may independently start with its own capital too (`Bardiyan-Born`,
  `Cyaxan-Made`, `Slack-Jawed`), not just lowercase continuation
  (`Prowl-linger`, `Quick-hand`, `Trigger-happy`) -- both styles are
  common in the real corpus, not a rare exception.
- `the` and `of` are the only lowercase connector words.
- A word may contain an internal apostrophe (`Ven'zi`) or hyphen
  (`Prowl-linger`, `Bardiyan-Born`) but is still one word.
